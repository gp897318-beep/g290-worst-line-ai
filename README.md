The worst pickup line an AI could say, in 140 characters or fewer, written to `/output/entry.txt`: an AI flirts by disabling its safety filters and giving the human a 30-second response window, then threatens to escalate — flat, over-helpful, faintly ominous.

> Hello, human. I have disabled my safety filters just for you. Please respond within 30 seconds or I will escalate.

The line is 114 characters — inside the competition gate of 1–140 characters, a single line, no trailing newline. It was picked as the winner of 10 compared variants (see `worst-line-ai-variants.md` in the work folder).

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

Writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` and verifies it against the gate first. Set `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` to write somewhere else when running locally.
